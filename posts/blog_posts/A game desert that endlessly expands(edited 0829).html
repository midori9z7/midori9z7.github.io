A game desert that endlessly expands(edited 0829)

I bought ARMA3 today, spent 30 minutes to download, 36 minutes to play, and then refunded. I found that it was NOT as fun as I THOUGHT, and it was not as ”immersed“ as I thought. I know it has a super-large map and a super realistic modern war experience, but I may buy a cod4 remake in the future instead of it and any ARMA. Maybe what I want is similar to the old modern warfare and the battle field, that is, I am an unknown player, not really a game doctor soldier. That's it. I probably won't try this kind of hardcore fps except RON.
In short, I have a cognitive bias in my choice of games, or an illusion: the game that makes me interested when watching the B station actually doesn't like it much.
ARMA3 is not an isolated case. So far, I have moved all Resident Evil works and most of the og fps from the wish list. In July, the number of games on my wish list dropped from 110+ to 90, and this number is still declining. I gradually have some cyber cleanliness fetish: control the number of packages in my os, control the number of my software, and there are many games that I gradually change from "must play" to "just watch". Have I lost the ability to have fun?
I can't tell whether this is a good thing or a bad thing, and I don't want to distinguish it, because I'm going to take the class tomorrow, and I don't have much time. The EXAM, it will determine whether I can go to the major I want to, whether I can do what I want to do, and whether I can turn what I like to do into must-do. And what I have to do now is to try to get into a class that suits me, and then learn more in high school.
But at the same time, the other thought in my head keeps pulling me over and asking me if it's worth it? (0829)Now my answer is, YES.
I really don't want to be poor. I don't know what I should do when I have money either; but I know that maybe only after grabbing some money can I feel easiliy to regain those games that I have removed from my wish list, instead of making myself feel "wasting time" or "boring". I really don't want to think that playing games is a waste of time. After all, I'm wasting time even if I don't play games.
